## Overview

CodeCoogs uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on our website and application. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can control them.

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, phone, tablet) that contain information about your browsing activities. They help websites remember your preferences and provide a better user experience.

## Types of Cookies We Use

### 1. Essential Cookies (Required)

These cookies are necessary for the basic functionality of our Service:
- **Session cookies** - Keep you logged in while using our app
- **Authentication cookies** - Verify your identity and security

### 2. Preference Cookies (Optional)

These cookies remember your choices:
- **Theme preference** (`coco-theme`) - Remembers whether you prefer light, dark, or system theme
  - Storage: Browser localStorage and cookies (365-day expiration)
  - Values: "system", "light", "dark", "latte", "pink-sorbet", "frappe", "macchiato", "mocha"

## Third-Party Cookies

Third-party services may set cookies:
- **Stripe** - For secure payment processing
- **Discord** - For OAuth authentication
- **Google Calendar** - For event integration

We recommend reviewing their cookie policies:
- [Stripe Privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy)
- [Discord Privacy](https://discord.com/privacy)
- [Google Privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Your Cookie Choices

### How to Control Cookies

**In Your Browser:**
1. Open your browser's Settings or Preferences
2. Look for "Privacy," "Security," or "Cookies"
3. You can:
   - Block all cookies
   - Block third-party cookies only
   - Clear existing cookies
   - Allow specific sites to set cookies

**For CodeCoogs Specifically:**
- All non-essential cookies (like theme preference) can be disabled without affecting core functionality
- Your browser settings will override any cookies we try to set

### Important Note

If you disable essential cookies, you may not be able to:
- Log in to your account
- Access certain features
- Use payment functionality

## Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" feature. CodeCoogs respects this signal, though third-party services may not.

## Local Storage

In addition to cookies, we use browser local storage to remember:
- Your theme preference
- Any other user preferences you set

You can clear local storage through your browser's developer tools or by clearing "Browsing Data."
