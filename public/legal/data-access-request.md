# Data Access & Deletion Requests

**Effective Date:** August 7, 2026

CodeCoogs respects your rights regarding your personal data. This document explains how to request access to your data, correct inaccurate information, or request deletion.

## Your Data Rights

Under privacy laws and our commitment to transparency, you have the right to:

1. **Access Your Data** - Receive a copy of all personal information we hold about you
2. **Correct Your Data** - Fix inaccurate or incomplete information
3. **Delete Your Data** - Request deletion of your account and associated data
4. **Export Your Data** - Receive your data in a portable format
5. **Opt-Out** - Unsubscribe from non-essential communications

## How to Submit a Request

### Option 1: Self-Service (In-App)

The easiest way is through your account settings:
1. Log in to your CodeCoogs account
2. Go to **Settings** → **Account**
3. Choose:
   - **Download My Data** - Export all your personal information
   - **Update Profile** - Correct any information
   - **Delete Account** - Permanently remove your account

### Option 2: Email Request

Send a detailed email to: **[contact email]**

In your email, please include:
- Your full name
- Email address associated with your account
- Type of request (access, correction, deletion, export)
- Specific details about what you're requesting

**Example:**
```
Subject: Data Access Request

Hello,

I am requesting a copy of all personal data CodeCoogs holds about me.

Name: John Smith
Email: john.smith@uh.edu
Student ID: [if you have it]

Thank you
```

## Response Timeline

We will respond to your request within **7 business days**. If your request is complex, we may need an additional 7 days to gather all information.

## What Data We Can Provide

When you request your data, we will provide:

**Personal Profile Information:**
- First and last name
- Email address
- Phone number (if provided)
- Academic classification and major
- Expected graduation date
- Cougar Net ID
- Discord username (if linked)
- Profile photo

**Activity Data:**
- Event attendance records with dates
- Points earned and point history
- Leaderboard rankings by academic year
- Form responses and submissions
- Support tickets and messages
- File uploads

**Account Data:**
- Account creation date
- Last login date
- Login history (if available)
- Connected third-party accounts (Discord, Google Calendar)

**Financial Data (if applicable):**
- Payment history
- Membership status
- Billing information
- Refund records

## What We Cannot Provide

We cannot provide:
- Internal administrative notes or comments
- Raw server logs (except as required by law)
- Data of other users
- Backup copies of deleted data

## Data Deletion Process

### What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When you request account deletion:

**Immediately Deleted:**
- Your password and authentication credentials
- Your profile photo and uploads
- Your linked third-party accounts (Discord, Google Calendar)

**Deleted Within 30 Days:**
- All personal profile information
- Event attendance records
- Points and leaderboard entries
- Form responses
- Support tickets
- Any unfinished transactions

**Retained (as required by law/contract):**
- Financial/payment records (7 years, required by law)
- Aggregate usage statistics (anonymized)
- Legal records if required

**Shared with Third Parties:**
We will notify Stripe of account deletion for payment records. Discord and Google will retain their own copies according to their retention policies.

### Important Notes About Deletion

- Deletion is **permanent and irreversible**
- Any points, rankings, or event credits will be removed
- You will need to create a new account if you want to rejoin
- We cannot retrieve data after 30 days

## Data Correction

To update your personal information:

1. Log into your account
2. Go to **Settings** → **Profile**
3. Edit and save your changes

For data you cannot correct in-app (historical records, attendance), contact us via email with details about what needs to be corrected.

## Privacy Concerns

If you believe your privacy has been violated or your data has been misused:

1. Contact us immediately at **[contact email]**
2. Include:
   - Description of the incident
   - When you became aware of it
   - How it affects you
   - Any evidence you have

We take privacy concerns seriously and will investigate promptly.

## Children & Minors

If you are under 18, your parent or guardian may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. We may require proof of guardianship.
